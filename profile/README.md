# 🔬 DRMC Science Club

### Explore • Experiment • Innovate

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of **DRMC Science Club**, Dhaka Residential Model College.

DRMC Science Club is dedicated to fostering scientific curiosity, innovation, research, problem-solving, and collaboration among students. We provide opportunities for students to explore science beyond the classroom and develop their constructive, exploratory, and inventive abilities.

## 🎯 Our Objectives

- Encourage the pursuit of scientific knowledge and broaden students' scientific outlook.
- Develop constructive, exploratory, and inventive abilities among students.
- Create interest in the latest scientific inventions and discoveries.
- Encourage awareness of scientific facts and events in our surroundings.
- Develop mathematical and scientific problem-solving skills.
- Connect with schools, colleges, science clubs, and the wider scientific community.
- Encourage participation in science competitions, quizzes, Olympiads, and collaborative activities.

## 🚀 What We Do

- 🔬 **Science & Research**
- 🤖 **Robotics & Engineering**
- 💻 **Programming & Technology**
- 🧮 **Mathematics & Problem Solving**
- 🧠 **Olympiads & Quizzing**
- 🏆 **Science Festivals & Competitions**
- 🛠️ **Student Projects & Innovation**

## 🏆 Events & Activities

DRMC Science Club organizes and participates in various scientific activities, including:

- Inter-school and inter-college science festivals
- Internal science quizzes and Olympiads
- Scientific discussions and workshops
- Mathematics and problem-solving activities
- Robotics and technology projects
- Inter-school and inter-college quizzes and competitions

## 💻 Open Source & Projects

This GitHub organization serves as the home for software, robotics, research, and other technical projects developed and maintained by **DRMC Science Club** and its members.

Our repositories document our projects, encourage collaboration, and help future members continue building upon the work of previous teams.
